Positive suction head

Suction head exists when the total suction head is 
above atmospheric pressure. Total suction head is 
the reading of a gauge at the suction of the pump, 
converted to feet of liquid, and referred to datum plus 
the velocity head at the point of gauge attachment.

Velocity head

The acceptable vibration level of a centrifugal pump
depends on the rigidity of the pump and the 
supporting structure. Recommended values for 
vibration can vary between .20 ips velocity to .60 ips 
velocity depending on the operating characteristics 
and the structure. Refer to the standards of the 
"Hydraulic Institute" for the complete description 
and charts on various structures.
