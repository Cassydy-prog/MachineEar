24. Obstruction in liquid passages.
Dismantle pump and inspect passages of impeller and casing. 
Remove obstruction.

May be possible to over rate pump to point where it will provide
adequate pressure despite condition. Better to provide gas 
separation chamber on suction line near pump, and periodically 
exhaust accumulated gas. See item 14.

liquid to pressure in suction line.
Watch for bubble formation.)

26. Too small impeller diameter.
(Probable cause if none of above.)
See item 20.
