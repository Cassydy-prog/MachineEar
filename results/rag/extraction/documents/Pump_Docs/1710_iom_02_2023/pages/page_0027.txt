6  Maintenance

Checking the bearing seals
Check correct seating of axial seal rings mounted
on the shaft. Only gentle contact of the sealing lip
shall be established.

Check for quiet running
Check frequently for quiet running of the pump with
vibration measurements tools.

Check for leakage of the mechanical seal. Replace the mechanical seal if leakage is found.

6.3  Disassemble and replace the pump parts

For more information about spare parts and assembly and disassembly of the pump, contact
the local sales and service representative.
