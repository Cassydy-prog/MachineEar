4.8  Discharge pipeline

It is recommended that a non-return valve is assembled to the pumps discharge. The
installation of a gate valve after the non-return valve is beneficial to aid in priming of the
pump, trimming of the discharge flow and maintenance on the non return valve.

Discharge pipeline should be selected of a size applicable to carry the required capacity,
such that the friction head created is not too much. When the pipeline is laid over undulating
ground by using high points where air pockets can form, vent cocks must be put to expel the
accumulation of air, which will affect the discharge capacity of the pump.

4.9  Coupling arrangement

After mounting to the foundation and the connection of the piping, the coupling must be
adjusted again, even if the unit was delivered completely mounted on the frame.

The following is intended as a guide only for the correct selection of the appropriate
coupling .

• Type of pump
• Type of drive
• The maximum output power/shaft power of the drive
• Duty type: Continuous or Intermittent
• Shaft diameter & key size of pump & drive
• Alignment: Refer to coupling manufactures specifications.
Contact the local sales and service representative for any requests or information.

NOTICE:
Improper alignment of the unit can lead to damages at coupling and unit.

4.10  Electrical installation

1. Remove the screws of the terminal box cover.
2. Connect and fasten the power cables according to the applicable wiring diagram.
a) Connect the ground (earth) lead.

Make sure that the ground (earth) lead is longer than the phase leads.
b) Connect the phase leads.
3. Mount the terminal box cover.

NOTICE:
Tighten the cable glands carefully to ensure protection against cable slipping and
humidity entering the terminal box.
