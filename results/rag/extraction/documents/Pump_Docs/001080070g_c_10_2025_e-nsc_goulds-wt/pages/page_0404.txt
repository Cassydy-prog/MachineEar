of the Directive 2012/19/EU of the European 
Parliament and of the Council of 4 July 2012 on 
waste electrical and electronic equipment

product at the end of its useful life must be 
collected separately and not disposed of 
together with other mixed urban waste.

negative effects on the environment and on

of the materials of which the equipment is 
composed.
